THE WEIMAR REPUBLIC
Post-Publication Document — Clarifications, Errata, and Addendum
Updated 16 October, 2026
Changes since the last update are shown in red.
Need help with something that isn’t addressed here? Please post to BGG The Weimar Republic.
	Rulebook
p. 10, 2.0 Setup Diagram
Errata: The Coalition faction mat shows a Reform marker; it should show the Reformation marker.
p. 12, 3.2 General Election Victory
Clarification: The number of PV that RC must transfer to steal a General Election victory is half of the NSDAP’s PV before the transfer takes place.
p. 13, 4.0 Sequence of Play, 4. Advance Timeline Step
Clarification: When returning Assassination Markers to available boxes, the Momentum faction decides where each goes.
p. 13, 4.0 Sequence of Play, 6. Held Card Penalty
Errata: In scenarios that go through 1933, all factions pay the Held Card penalty to remove Influence.
p. 22, 6.0 Limitations on Actions & Events
Clarification: Both the Assault Action and the Place Assassinations Action may remove Influence cubes from spaces where Influence was placed in the same Acton step, and vice versa (i.e., order does not matter).
Similarly, an Event that grants the Coalition a Place Influence Action is limited by the Economy track (5.5). However, an Event that allows the Colation to “place”, “add”, or “replace” Influence is not limited by the Economy track.
p. 24, 6.1 Assaults, Apply Assault Hits
Clarification: In Assaults against KPD, a single hit is not sufficient to remove an Uprising, and so is wasted and may not be applied to remove any KPD Influence in the Assault space.


p. 29, 6.6 RC Actions, Place Leverage on a Track
Errata: Disregard “, adjusting the latter if appropriate.” Placing Black Leverage on the Economy track does not adjust it.
p. 33, 8.1 When Not the Active Faction and 8.2 Important Non-player Terms
Clarification: When an NP faction is not the active faction and targets an opposing faction or requires an impulse space, draw one of its own NP cards.
p. 34, 8.3 Non-player Victory
Clarification: Only NP Coalition may win via a Sudden Victory [3.1], and only during the Golden Twenties or Decline era. All other NP factions may only win via a General Election [3.2], Event Card [3.3], or Scenario End [3.4] Victory.
p. 34, 8.4 New Year Step
Clarification: If the bot card used to determine the turn order for the upcoming year includes the Reshuffle Bot Deck instruction, reshuffle the bot deck.
p. 43, Comprehensive Non-player Example of Play
Errata: The graphic that shows Coalition influence being placed in a Pommern should show only one Coalition influence cube present already, not two.

Errata: The event card Berlin Riots, given to the Coalition Player, is card #27, not #24.

Errata: The KPD Move Middle Class Sympathies action does not depend on the number of KPD Cadres on the map.
